Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to trick users into sharing sensitive information.

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices, steal information, or disrupt systems.

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or performing unsafe actions.

Phishing Awareness

Before clicking a suspicious link:

  • Check the sender's address.
  • Look carefully at the website domain.
  • Do not share passwords or verification codes.
  • Be careful with unexpected attachments.
  • When unsure, verify the message through an official source.

Safe Browsing Tips

✔ Keep your browser and operating system updated.

✔ Use trusted websites and check the domain name.

✔ Avoid downloading files from unknown sources.

✔ Do not enter sensitive information on suspicious websites.

Password Security

Use long, unique passwords for your accounts and avoid reusing the same password across different services.

  • Use a mixture of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Avoid easily guessed information.
  • Consider using a password manager.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication when available.
